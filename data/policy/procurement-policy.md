# Procurement Policy — Purchase Requisition Splitting

**Applies to:** all purchase requisitions (PRs) and purchase orders (POs) raised by employees.
**Owner:** Procurement · **Version:** 1.0

## 1. Approval matrix

Every requirement is approved at the tier that matches its **total value**.

| Tier | Total value (INR) | Approver | Quotes required | Competitive bid (RFQ) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | below 1,00,000 | Line Manager | 1 | No |
| T2 | 1,00,000 to below 5,00,000 | Department Head | 3 | No |
| T3 | 5,00,000 to below 25,00,000 | CFO | 3 | Yes |
| T4 | 25,00,000 and above | CFO and Procurement Committee | 3 | Yes |

## 2. Splitting is prohibited

Dividing a single requirement into several requisitions or orders, so that each one falls below an approval tier or avoids the quote or competitive-bid requirement, is prohibited. This applies whether the split is made by one employee or by several employees acting together.

## 3. What counts as a single requirement

Requisitions are treated as **one requirement** when any of the following is true:

1. They serve the same project, event or business purpose.
2. They are for the same goods or services from the same vendor, raised within a short period (normally within 30 days).
3. The items are components or accessories of each other, for example laptops, their chargers and their docking stations for the same team.
4. They are delivered to the same site in the same delivery window for the same need.
5. They are raised by different employees in the same cost centre for the same need from the same vendor.

The **lookback window** for comparing requisitions is **30 days**.

## 4. Legitimate exceptions

The following are **not** splitting, provided the facts in the requisitions support them:

| Code | Exception | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| RECURRING | Recurring consumables | Routine, periodic purchases (stationery, pantry, cleaning, courier) from a vendor designated as recurring, at normal periodic volumes |
| BLANKET_PO | Blanket PO call-offs | Each requisition references a valid blanket PO for the same vendor, within its validity dates and approved amount |
| SEPARATE_PROJECT | Separate requirements | Different projects, cost centres, sites **and** business purposes, each documented on the requisition |
| EMERGENCY | Documented emergency | Requisitions flagged as emergency with a specific reason (for example equipment breakdown, safety issue) |
| STAGGERED_DELIVERY | Vendor-agreed staggered delivery | One requirement delivered in instalments, where the total was approved at the correct tier |

A justification must be **specific** and consistent with the requisition data. General reasons such as "urgent", "needed quickly" or "we always order this way" do not qualify.

## 5. Consequence

When requisitions are found to be one requirement:

1. They are consolidated and routed to the approver for the **combined** value.
2. Quote and competitive-bid requirements apply to the combined value.
3. Repeated splitting by the same employee (three or more confirmed cases within 90 days) is reported to Internal Audit.
